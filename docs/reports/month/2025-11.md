## Top 10 Learnings
1. **Small habits can significantly reduce anxiety and overwhelm.** Implementing tiny daily habits, such as the 10-Minute Rule, can help restore inner calm and improve overall mental health. (Source: Harvard’s 30-Year Research Reveals — article, November)
2. **Neuroplasticity can be harnessed through discomfort.** Engaging in uncomfortable actions, like public speaking or cold showers, rewires the brain and strengthens neural pathways. (Source: How to be extremely disciplined — article, November)
3. **Writing exercises can enhance problem-solving abilities.** A 20-minute writing exercise activates the prefrontal cortex and primes the brain to notice opportunities, making it easier to solve complex problems. (Source: The 20-Minute Writing Exercise That Neuroscientists Say Can Solve Your Hardest Problems — article, November)
4. **Authentic visibility is more effective than self-promotion.** Focusing on sharing expertise and value rather than seeking praise can lead to more meaningful connections and professional growth. (Source: How to Build a Personal Brand When You’re a Senior Professional Who Hates Self Promotion — article, November)
5. **AI's exponential growth may not lead to reduced human tasks.** The real question is whether AI improvements will translate into fewer tasks for humans, a historical first. (Source: Thoughts by a non-economist on AI and economics — article, November)
6. **The importance of articulating thoughts clearly.** Effective communication hinges on fluency and coherence, which can be improved through structured delivery and anticipation of questions. (Source: How to articulate your thoughts more clearly than 99% of people — podcast, November)
7. **Visibility requires consistency, but self-promotion is exhausting.** Establishing a routine for content creation can alleviate the stress of self-promotion while maintaining visibility. (Source: How to Build a Personal Brand When You’re a Senior Professional Who Hates Self Promotion — article, November)
8. **AI-driven automation is becoming the standard expectation.** Customers now anticipate zero-touch workflows, which are essential for competitive advantage in financial services. (Source: The CFO Dashboard; Ramp, Brex or Mercury - 18 Months Later — article, November)
9. **Personal data restraint may cost competitive advantage.** Google's Gemini prioritizes user safety and control over extensive personalization, which could hinder its market position. (Source: Google Has Your Data. Gemini Barely Uses It. — article, November)
10. **Curiosity is essential for intellectual growth.** Engaging in reading and thinking out loud fosters curiosity, which enhances the ability to ask better questions and synthesize information. (Source: How to become dangerously articulate — article, November)
