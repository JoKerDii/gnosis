## Top 10 Learnings
**AI governance must be embedded in execution layers** — For organizations evaluating agentic AI, it's crucial that governance is integrated within the execution layer rather than existing as a separate policy document. This approach can help prevent AI from bypassing important ethical considerations. (Source: ServiceNow resolves 90% of its own IT requests autonomously. Now it wants to do the same for any enterprise — article, March)

**The gig economy is reshaping income sources for millennials** — Nearly one-third of millennials now rely on gig work and tips as their primary income, highlighting a shift in how younger generations earn and manage finances. This has increased demand for instant payment solutions. (Source: One-Third of Millennials Now Rely on Gig Payments and Tips as Primary Income — article, March)

**AI's impact on labor markets is nuanced** — Despite the potential for AI to automate many tasks, actual observed exposure in the workforce is significantly lower than theoretical capabilities, with no systematic increase in unemployment for highly exposed workers since late 2022. (Source: Labor market impacts of AI: A new measure and early evidence — article, March)

**Agentic payments require universal infrastructure** — The current race among companies like Google, Visa, and Mastercard to establish agentic payment systems risks creating fragmented ecosystems. A universal bridge is needed for AI to interact seamlessly with various payment providers. (Source: Visa, Mastercard and Google are building agentic payments. None are solving the real problem — article, March)

**AI models are becoming integral to enterprise workflows** — OpenAI's focus on helping businesses build and manage agents signifies a shift from novelty to practical applications of AI in enterprise processes, emphasizing the importance of voice as a modality in markets like India. (Source: OpenAI COO says ‘we have not yet really seen AI penetrate enterprise business processes’ — article, March)

**AI's reliability gaps pose business risks** — Many organizations using AI have faced negative consequences due to inaccuracies, indicating that achieving high reliability is essential for successful AI integration into business processes. (Source: Karpathy’s March of Nines shows why 90% AI reliability isn’t even close to enough — article, March)

**The importance of AI in physical environments is growing** — AI is moving beyond data centers into physical environments, indicating a trend toward integrating AI into everyday operational tasks, which could redefine industries. (Source: AI is rapidly moving out of the data center and into physical environments — podcast, March)

**AI agents will initially rely on traditional payment methods** — The evolution of payments for AI agents suggests that virtual cards will be used first, with stablecoins playing a complementary role for speed and programmability in autonomous transactions. (Source: Agents will use cards first. Then stablecoins — article, March)

**AI is reshaping public opinion dynamics** — The potential for AI to converge on "expert opinion" risks marginalizing diverse viewpoints, which could lead to a homogenized public discourse. (Source: How AI Will Reshape Public Opinion — article, March)

**The AI value models are redefining business strategies** — OpenAI's five distinct AI value models emphasize the need for businesses to rethink their strategies around AI, focusing on workforce empowerment and process re-engineering rather than just cost savings. (Source: The five AI value models driving business reinvention — article, March)
