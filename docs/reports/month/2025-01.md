## Top 10 Learnings
1. **AI-Generated Content Risks Manipulation** — AI's potential to create hyper-targeted entertainment raises concerns about cognitive manipulation and addiction, particularly as companies like Meta advance in generative AI. 2. **Stages of Grief in AGI Development** — The emotional responses to AGI development can be framed through the Kübler-Ross model, highlighting that denial, anger, bargaining, depression, and acceptance are common reactions. 5. **AI Tools Favor Experienced Developers** — Current AI coding tools primarily assist experienced developers, highlighting a 'knowledge paradox' where AI benefits those already skilled rather than novices. 6. **AI's Impact on Job Structures** — AI agents can unbundle and rebundle roles within organizations, altering power dynamics and job scopes.
