3. **Blockchain's potential is still largely untapped** — While blockchain technology promises to revolutionize transaction costs and efficiency, widespread adoption is hindered by institutional inertia and technological challenges. 7. **The future of AI lies in multimodality** — The trend towards multimodal AI, which integrates various data types (text, images, audio), is becoming essential for developing more capable AI systems.
