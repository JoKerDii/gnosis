2. **Systems Thinking** — A holistic approach to problem-solving that emphasizes understanding the interconnections and complexities within systems, useful for strategic leadership. (Source: Today’s Most Crucial Leadership Skill Is Systems Thinking — article, April)

4. **Digital Hygiene** — Practices aimed at protecting personal data and maintaining online security in an age of pervasive data collection and internet fraud. (Source: Digital hygiene — article, April)

5. **AI Interpretability** — The principle that AI systems should be understandable and transparent to ensure safe and ethical deployment, becoming increasingly critical in AI development. (Source: The Urgency of Interpretability — article, April)

6. **Hybrid Roles** — Job positions that combine technical skills with human-centric abilities, reflecting the changing landscape of work in response to AI advancements. (Source: The Jobs That Will Fall First As AI Takes Over The Workplace — article, April)

7. **Adaptability** — The ability to adjust to new conditions and challenges, crucial for career resilience in a rapidly changing job market influenced by AI. (Source: How to future-proof your career in the age of AI — article, April)

8. **Collective Intelligence** — The shared or group intelligence that emerges from collaboration and collective efforts, increasingly valued in leadership and organizational contexts. (Source: Curation is the new leadership superpower — article, April)

9. **Job Automation** — The process by which jobs are increasingly performed by machines or AI, leading to significant changes in the workforce landscape. (Source: The Jobs That Will Fall First As AI Takes Over The Workplace — article, April)
