## Top 10 Learnings
**AI's reasoning capabilities are simulated, not genuine** — Current evidence indicates that large language models (LLMs) simulate reasoning rather than truly understanding it, necessitating careful evaluation of their use in complex decision-making contexts. This realization has shifted my perspective on the reliability of AI in critical applications. (Source: Can Large Language Models Reason? — article, November)

**Finetuning in LLMs is nuanced and context-dependent** — Finetuning all layers of a language model isn't always necessary; focusing on the last token can capture more contextual information. This insight reshapes how I approach model training, emphasizing efficiency and targeted adjustments. (Source: Building A GPT-Style LLM Classifier From Scratch — article, October)

**AI's integration into organizations requires hands-on experimentation** — Successful AI implementation often stems from user-driven innovation rather than top-down strategies. This realization has shifted my perspective on AI adoption, highlighting the importance of grassroots experimentation. (Source: AI in organizations: Some tactics — article, October)

**The importance of continuous improvement in teamwork** — Establishing a system for real-time measurement and continuous improvement can significantly enhance team performance. This has influenced my approach to team management, advocating for structured feedback loops. (Source: New Rules for Teamwork — article, October)

**AI can enhance e-commerce through hyper-personalization** — AI-driven recommendation systems during Cyber Week demonstrated significant improvements in customer engagement and sales conversions. This insight has influenced my approach to integrating AI tools in marketing strategies. (Source: E-Commerce Unleashed — article, December)

## Top 10 Concepts
**Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** — A model architecture that combines language generation with external data retrieval to enhance accuracy and context relevance. It is applicable when integrating real-time information into AI responses. (Source: Advanced RAG Techniques: an Illustrated Overview — article, October)

**Hyper-Personalization** — The use of AI to tailor user experiences and recommendations based on individual preferences, enhancing customer engagement in e-commerce. (Source: E-Commerce Unleashed — article, December)

**KV Cache** — A memory optimization technique in transformer models that allows for efficient attention computation by caching keys and values, crucial for scaling LLMs. (Source: Transformer inference tricks — article, December)

**Agent Engine Optimization (AEO)** — A framework for optimizing interactions with AI agents rather than traditional SEO, focusing on efficiency and task completion. This concept emerged in discussions about the future of web design and AI integration. (Source: Agent-Responsive Design: Rethinking the web for an agentic future — article, November)

**Organizational Dysfunction Framework** — A categorization of common dysfunctions within leadership teams, including hyper-competition and complacency. This framework aids in diagnosing and addressing team issues. (Source: Why Leadership Teams Fail — article, October)
