Top 10 Concepts
**Generative AI customization** — The practice of tailoring generative AI models to meet specific business needs, enhancing their effectiveness and relevance. This concept emerged in discussions about the challenges of implementing generative AI effectively. (Source: The state of AI in early 2024 — article, June)

**Sovereign AI** — A national strategy to develop and control AI capabilities within a country, ensuring data sovereignty and security. This concept surfaced in discussions about global tech competition and national security concerns. (Source: Nvidia’s New Sales Booster — article, June)

**Model commoditization** — The process by which AI models become standardized and widely available, leading to increased competition and reduced differentiation among providers. This concept is crucial for understanding the evolving landscape of AI technologies. (Source: Model commoditization and product moats - Interconnects — article, May)

**AI ethics framework** — A set of guidelines and principles designed to ensure the ethical development and deployment of AI technologies. This concept is gaining importance as organizations navigate the complexities of AI integration. (Source: Is OpenAI an AI Surveillance Tool? — article, June)

**Prompt engineering** — The art of crafting inputs for AI models to elicit desired outputs, crucial for maximizing the effectiveness of generalist AI systems. This concept is essential for developers looking to leverage AI capabilities fully. (Source: The Power of Prompting — article, May)
